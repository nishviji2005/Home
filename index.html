UG Courses



📅 4 YEARS

Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering is a professional Engineering discipline that
deals with the design, construction, and maintenance of the
physical and naturally built environment, including works like
bridges, roads, canals, dams, and buildings.



📅 4 YEARS

Computer Science Engineering

Computer engineering, also known as Information technology
engineering, information engineering methodology or data
engineering, is a software engineering approach to designing
and developing information systems.



📅 4 YEARS

Electronics and Communication Engineering

Electronics and Communications Engineering (ECE) involves
researching, designing developing and testing of electronic
equipment used in various systems.



📅 4 YEARS

Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering is a field of Engineering that generally
deals with the study and application of electricity,
electronics and electromagnetism. The field first became an identifiable
occupation in the late nineteenth century



📅 4 YEARS

Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical engineering is an engineering branch that
combines engineering physics and mathematics principles
with materials science to design, analyze, manufacture, and
maintain mechanical systems.



📅 4 YEARS

Computer Science and Business System

The department offers a B.Tech. in Computer Science and
Business Systems to develop professionally competent
engineers with good business insights. This is an overwhelming
career option for today's technologists.

PG Courses



📅 2 YEARS

M.E Construction Engineering And Management

M.E. Construction Engineering and Management is
a postgraduate structural engineering course. Construction
Engineering and Management is a professional discipline that
deals with the designing, planning, construction and
management of huge infrastructures.The applicants who
possess skills like creativity, problem solving, analyzing complex data etc.



📅 2 YEARS

M.E-Thermal Engineering

Thermal engineers design systems which provide heating or
cooling, using thermodynamics, that transfer heat or energy
through liquids and gases. They also construct these systems,
and perform test to ensure proper functionality.The energy can
be transformed between two mediums or transferred into
other forms of energy.It is the branch of production, and operation of machinery.



📅 2 YEARS

Master of Business Administration

MBA program cover various areas of business administration
such as accounting, applied statistics, human resources,
business communication, business ethics, business law,
strategic management, business strategy, finance, managerial
economics, management, entrepreneurship, marketing,
supply-chain management, and operations management in a
manner most relevant to management analysis and strategy.
